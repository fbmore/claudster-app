1. The app

Claudster shows the state of your Claude Code and Codex sessions on your Mac and on your other devices, and lets you queue tasks for those sessions. The Mac app is free, as a download from this website; it is not distributed through the Mac App Store, and it is all you need. The companion app for iPhone and iPad is free on the App Store, and every feature works free on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac. Reaching your Mac from anywhere else, through Claudster's relay, is part of Claudster Pro, an optional purchase in that app (see section 2).

2. Claudster Pro

  • What it includes. Claudster Pro lets the iPhone and iPad app reach your Mac away from its Wi-Fi, through Claudster's relay, end-to-end encrypted. Everything else, including every feature on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac and the whole Mac app, stays free.
  • Plans. Monthly and yearly subscriptions, and a one-time lifetime purchase. The app shows each plan's price and period, in your currency, before you buy.
  • Payment. Purchases are made through Apple and charged to your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.
  • Renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Free trial. When offered, the yearly plan starts with a free week. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly price is charged. Buying another plan during the trial ends the trial.
  • Lifetime. A one-time purchase that doesn't renew, for as long as we offer Claudster's relay.
  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restoring. Choose Restore Purchases in the app's Settings to restore Pro on another iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Fair use. To keep the relay working for everyone, it closes links left open (15 minutes without use, or 2 hours connected), limits each Mac to a fair daily amount (currently 6 hours or 2 GB), and may pause for maintenance. On the same Wi-Fi as your Mac, nothing is limited.
