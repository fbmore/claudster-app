Every Claude Code and Codex session, on one page.

A dashboard for macOS that shows which sessions are working, which are done, and which are waiting on you, and lets you answer each one without hunting for its terminal. It lives in your menu bar, and opens on your iPhone and iPad too.

Know which one needs you

Start work in three projects, step away, and find out in five seconds which one is blocked and what it wants.

Wed 9:41

Waiting on you, first

Sessions blocked on a question or a permission go to the top, with the question itself.

On your iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app finds your Mac through your Keychain, at home or away. Share to Claudster from any app, and type / for the session's commands.

Back to the terminal

Focus brings the right Terminal or iTerm tab forward. Queue a follow-up from the card, and edit it until the session picks it up.

Every project looks like itself

Cards wear each project's app icon and brand color, found in the repo. Screenshots of the work, when you want them.

Menu bar and Dock

The mascot sleeps while everything is done and wakes when a session is busy or needs you. Its menu lists every project; click one to jump to its terminal.

Ask several at once

Ask all sends one request to every project you pick, each to its own session. Handy for “bump the version” or “is the site up to date?”

Show, don't describe

Preview a project's site, pick an element, sketch or mark up a screenshot and blur anything private, then send it to the session.

Codex, too

OpenAI's Codex CLI sessions share the board, with the same hooks and status skill. Install them in Settings › Claude Code.

Private by design

No account and no analytics. Everything stays on your Mac; the relay and alerts that reach your phone are end-to-end encrypted, so they pass through without being read.

Set up in a minute

  1. Download and openDrag Claudster to Applications. It's signed and notarized, and lives in the menu bar.
  2. Install the hooksSettings › Claude Code › Install, and Codex just below if you use it. Sessions already running start reporting, with no restart.
  3. Get back to workLeave the room. Claudster tells you when something needs you.
