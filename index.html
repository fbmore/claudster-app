Every Claude Code session, on one page.

A dashboard for macOS that shows which sessions are working, which are done, and which are waiting on you, and lets you answer each one without hunting for its terminal. It lives in your menu bar, and opens on your phone too.

Download for Mac macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

iPhone app · TestFlight coming soon

See how it works · Watch the videos

Know which one needs you

Start work in three projects, step away, and find out in five seconds which one is blocked and what it wants.

Wed 9:41

Waiting on you, first

Sessions blocked on a question or a permission go to the top, with the question itself.

On your phone

Scan a QR code and the same live page opens on your phone, over your own Wi‑Fi.

Back to the terminal

Focus brings the right Terminal or iTerm tab forward. Send a follow-up from the card and the session picks it up.

Every project looks like itself

Cards wear each project's app icon and brand color, found in the repo. Screenshots of the work, when you want them.

Menu bar and Dock

The mascot sleeps while everything is done and wakes when a session is busy or needs you. Its menu lists every project; click one to jump to its terminal.

Private by design

The app has no account, no analytics and no servers of ours. Everything stays on your Mac, and your phone reaches it only with your private link.

See it in action

Two short films, 35 seconds each. The projects shown are demo data.

Set up in a minute

  1. Download and openDrag Claudster to Applications. It's signed and notarized, and lives in the menu bar.
  2. Install the hooksSettings › Claude Code › Install. Sessions already running start reporting, with no restart.
  3. Get back to workLeave the room. Claudster tells you when something needs you.
